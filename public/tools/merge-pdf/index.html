About & Privacy

Built so your documents stay yours.

NoUploadPDF processes files inside your browser memory. When you pair your phone via QR code, photos go peer-to-peer over an encrypted WebRTC DataChannel while a signaling relay sees only the connection handshake. Cross-tab scanner handoffs stage pages in temporary local IndexedDB storage on your device, which is cleared automatically upon import or when you clear the session.
